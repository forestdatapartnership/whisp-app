## Data Processing

### REGULAR DATA PROCESSING

Whisp allows the submission of geo-data, including vector geometries, which may be processed via Google Earth Engine (“GEE”), a third-party cloud service managed by Google. Data transmitted to GEE may be temporarily cached and processed in cloud-based data centres. FAO does not store or retain user-submitted geo-data.

### SENSITIVE DATA PROCESSING

Geometries submitted by the User and processed via GEE are not attributable to any identifiable individual. The processing of the User’s geo-data within Whisp is instead carried out on servers maintained in Europe. FAO will not store or retain users’ geo-data.

FAO protects user privacy when accessing geo-data by transmitting only approximated geographic information to GEE, along with additional masking data to prevent identification of the actual area of interest. Such information is used solely to facilitate processing and is not permanently stored or retained by GEE.

Tabular data linked to the User’s geographical information is only temporarily cached, is not permanently stored and is not shared with GEE at any stage of processing.
